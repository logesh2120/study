When is a Consulting Arborist Essential?

There are many situations where expert services are not just helpful—they’re essential for safety, legal compliance, and asset protection.

01

Inventories & Assessments

“You can’t manage what you don’t know.”

We perform full inventories for municipalities, schools, and corporate campuses to understand species health and environmental value.

Note: Accurate tree surveys are critical for development; inaccuracies can delay projects.
02

Tree Management Plans

Strategies based on health data and invasive pest threats. We collaborate with architects for long-term care guidance.

03

Construction Conservation

Involving us early protects roots and canopies during remodels. We provide site inspections to avoid costly structural mistakes.

04

Planting & Infrastructure

“Right Tree, Right Place.” We work with Engineers on green strategies for storm water and soil volumes.

05

Diagnosing Health Issues

Identifying fungal infections and structural cracks. We use advanced tools like resistographs and drones.

06

Tree Appraisals

Objective monetary valuation for insurance claims or legal disputes using CTLA industry standards.

07

Legal & Expert Witness

Science-backed evidence for cases involving ownership disputes. We provide written reports suitable for litigation.
